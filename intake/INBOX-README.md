# What to put in this folder

This is your academy's intake folder. Drag in **everything** — we read every item, show you an
inventory of what we found and what we'll do with each piece, and whatever is missing we draft
for your approval. Nothing you drop here is published; it informs the build, and only material
you later approve enters the course.

**Most valuable, roughly in order:**

1. **Brand kit** — logo files, colors, fonts; a `design.md` or brand guidelines PDF if you have
   one. *No brand kit yet? We'll propose an identity for your approval.*
2. **Photos & footage of your instructor(s)** — headshots, portraits, any existing video of the
   person teaching. We'll ask separately how much you want to be on camera and confirm releases
   before anything ships; nothing is published without your written OK.
3. **Product & service documentation** — decks, guides, spec sheets, one-pagers, your website
   exports. What you sell, in your own words.
4. **Protocols & SOPs** — how your teams actually work. Competency courses are built on the real
   workflow, not the brochure.
5. **Sample outputs** — 2–3 anonymized reports, results, or screenshots of the product in use.
   (Demo/sandbox access is even better — tell us how.)
6. **Webinars & recorded talks** — often the richest source material. Note who the speakers are;
   we'll confirm rights before reusing anything.
7. **Claims & compliance material** — language your marketing/legal team has approved, and
   anything explicitly prohibited.

Folders, loose files, duplicates — all fine. Don't clean it up for us; that's our job.
