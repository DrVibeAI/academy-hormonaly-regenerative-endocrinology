# GCLS accreditation review — what to expect

*Draft for Omar to use when briefing GCLS. Not sent to anyone.*

## What this is

GCLS will review its first course through the new Perceptors review room: **Hormones and Peptides for Skin** from Hormonaly
Academy. The course is finished before you see it. Every module, the final quiz and the tutor's prepared answers have been approved
by the medical reviewer, Dr. Fady Hannah-Shmouni, MD FRCPC, and every cited statement has been checked against its source.

You receive **one email**, from omar@hormonaly.ai, when the course is ready. It contains a course overview and one link.

## How to review

1. **Open the link** (hormonaly.perceptors.ai/review) and sign in with your GCLS email address. You receive a one-time sign-in
   link by email; there is no password. Access lasts 30 days.
2. **Overview.** What the course covers, who it is for, the automated checks and their results, and the GCLS review criteria.
3. **Walk the course.** Every module is open to you exactly as learners see it, including exercises and the final check.
4. **Evidence.** Every source, every checked claim, and the full report of the source check.
5. **Ask the course.** An assistant answers questions about the material, its sources and its checks, from the course record only,
   with references. It explains; it does not decide, and it says so when the record does not cover a question.
6. **Decision.** Accredit, or request changes with notes. Your decision is recorded exactly as you enter it. Nothing is presented
   as GCLS-accredited until that record exists.

An agent pre-review brief (a recommendation only) estimates about 45 minutes of reviewer time.

## What we need to agree with GCLS before the email

- **Who reviews.** Dr. Dominik Thor alone, or a second reviewer as well (each needs a seat in the room).
- **Accreditation ID format.** The decision form asks for an ID when accrediting (for example GCLS-2026-0002).
- **Credit hours.** Whether GCLS assigns hours, and how; the course runs about 3 hours of learning plus a 14-question final check.
- **Validity.** How long an accreditation lasts before the course must be re-reviewed (the form accepts an end date).
- **Certificate wording.** Once accredited, the certificate is issued by GCLS with Hormonaly Academy as co-brand and verified by
  Perceptors; confirm GCLS is content with that wording.
- **Changes after accreditation.** How GCLS wants to hear about later updates (for example new evidence or regulatory changes).

## What is not claimed

The course states that it is designed toward future CME accreditation and offers no credit today. Until GCLS decides, the course
and its certificate make no GCLS accreditation claim anywhere.
