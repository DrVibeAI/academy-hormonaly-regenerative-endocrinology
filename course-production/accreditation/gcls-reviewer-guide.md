# GCLS accreditation review — what to expect

*Draft for Omar to use when briefing GCLS. Not sent to anyone.*

## What this is

GCLS reviews courses from Perceptors academies in one place, the review hub at **https://review.perceptors.ai**. The first course
is **Hormones and Peptides for Skin** from Hormonaly Academy (version 1.1.0). The course is finished before you see it. Every
module, the practice activities, the final check and the tutor's prepared answers have been approved by the medical reviewer,
Dr. Fady Hannah-Shmouni, MD FRCPC, and every cited statement has been checked against its source.

You receive **one email** when a course is ready. It comes from **Perceptors &lt;review@perceptors.ai&gt;**, and replies go to
omar@hormonaly.ai. It contains a short briefing and one link to the review hub.

## How to review

1. **Open the link** to review.perceptors.ai and sign in with your GCLS email address. You receive a one-time sign-in link by
   email; there is no password. Access lasts 30 days.
2. **The hub** lists every course waiting for review. Each course opens with the agent's brief and its **scorecard**: the GCLS
   review standard (version 0.1, a draft of 13 criteria), with an assessment for each criterion (meets, concern, fails or not
   applicable), a one-line finding and the evidence it rests on. The agent prepares; you decide.
3. **Open as a learner** takes you into the course's review room, already signed in: overview, scorecard, every module exactly
   as learners see it (practice activities and the final check included), the evidence (every source, every checked claim and
   the full source-check report), questions, and your decision.
4. **Ask.** An assistant answers questions about the course, its sources and its checks, from the course record only, with
   references, and says when the record does not cover a question. The **review desk** (review.perceptors.ai/desk) takes
   questions across all courses.
5. **Let the agent fill in.** Tell the agent what you found and it drafts the scorecard rows and decision notes from your words,
   for you to check and change. It never records a decision.
6. **Decision.** Accredit, or request changes with notes. Your decision and your scorecard are recorded exactly as you enter
   them, with the version of the standard you reviewed against.

The agent's pre-review (a recommendation only) recommends accreditation with conditions and estimates about 45 minutes of
reviewer time.

## What we need to agree with GCLS before the email

- **The review standard.** Version 0.1 is a draft written from the criteria GCLS has described, with five open questions for
  GCLS. Confirm or amend it (Dr. Dominik Thor).
- **Who reviews.** Dr. Dominik Thor alone, or a second reviewer as well (each needs access to the hub).
- **Accreditation ID format.** The decision form asks for an ID when accrediting (for example GCLS-2026-0002).
- **Credit hours.** Whether GCLS assigns hours, and how. The course runs about 4½ hours of learning (262 minutes, a planning
  estimate) plus a 14-question final check with an 80% pass mark.
- **Validity.** How long an accreditation lasts before the course must be re-reviewed (the form accepts an end date).
- **Certificate wording.** Once accredited, the certificate is issued by GCLS with Hormonaly Academy as co-brand and verified by
  Perceptors; confirm GCLS is content with that wording.
- **Changes after accreditation.** How GCLS wants to hear about later updates (for example new evidence or regulatory changes).
