## Findings to send back to the author with the final draft

1. **A&RE chapter 15, reference 4** — "Sikiric P, et al. Curr Med Chem 2018;25:1972" does not resolve on PubMed; the title matches *Curr Pharm Des* 2018;24:1990–2001 (PMID 29879879), which The Peptide Pocket Guide cites correctly.
2. **PPG-Aug, Argireline entry** — "Wang Y et al. (manufacturer follow-ups) [not indexed]": the 2013 *Am J Clin Dermatol* randomized placebo-controlled trial by Wang Y et al. is indexed (PMID 23417317).
3. **PPG-Aug, LL-37 entry** — Grönberg et al. is 2014 (printed 2015); Mahlapuu et al. is 2021 (printed 2022).
4. **A&RE chapter 15, RUO cautions** — the heavy-metal (arsenic, lead) contamination statement carries no citation and no primary analytical study of skin-peptide RUO products was found; the "30 % wrong sequence / 65 % endotoxin" figure that circulates online could not be traced to any journal. Claim `c-m04-16` is flagged.
5. **PPG-Aug, KPV entry** — "small open-label studies for atopic dermatitis" could not be located on PubMed.
6. **GHK-Cu regulatory status** — FDA's May 14, 2026 category document records the April 22, 2026 withdrawal, the May 5 partial reinstatement (non-injectable routes) and a PCAC consultation intended before end of February 2027. The guides' "Category 1 (non-injectable)" is right in substance but should be date-stamped.
7. **Not in either guide, worth adding** — Miller 2006 (the one indexed RCT of topical GHK-Cu), Aruan 2023 (independent RCT of Argireline vs pal-KTTKS), Esposito 2012 (TB-500 characterized as the Tβ4 17–23 fragment), the two 2026 BPC-157 trial registrations, and the two independent 2025–26 BPC-157 reviews.
