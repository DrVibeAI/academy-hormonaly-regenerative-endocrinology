## Mexico · Cenegenics · es-MX

**Verified framework.** COFEPRIS's [medicine register and current viewer](https://www.gob.mx/cofepris/articulos/cofepris-presenta-visor-de-registros-sanitarios-de-medicamentos) permits checks by active ingredient, brand and registration number and shows the registration's current state and therapeutic indication. COFEPRIS says [cosmetics are for surface contact](https://www.gob.mx/cofepris/acciones-y-programas/productos-cosmeticos-404020); an ingested, inhaled, injected or implanted substance is not a cosmetic. The [General Health Law amendment](https://dof.gob.mx/avisos/2045/SALUD070611/SALUD070611.htm) also bars attributing medicine-like disease claims to cosmetics. [COFEPRIS advertising records](https://www.gob.mx/cofepris/documentos/tramites-de-autorizacion-publicitaria) show that promotional authorization is a separate layer.

**Do not import US rules.** FDA approval, 503A/503B bulk lists and off-label doctrine in the source are explicitly US examples. The Mexican edition needs a COFEPRIS register check for the exact product, route and indication before any local status is stated. No Mexican approval or prohibition for a named peptide is inferred from a missing search result or another country's decision.

**Mexico-specific safety example for m03/m07.** [COFEPRIS's February 2026 tirzepatide alert](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/1055652/Alerta_Sanitaria_Tirzepatida_09022026.pdf), summarized by [Profeco](https://www.gob.mx/profeco/prensa/profeco-y-cofepris-alertan-por-comercializacion-ilegal-de-productos-con-tirzepatida?idiom=es-MX), names the unregistered Rapha, Dr. Pen and Peptide Xperts products sold online. This is a warning about those products, not a finding that all tirzepatide products lack Mexican registration. COFEPRIS also [warns against unsupervised semaglutide/liraglutide use and informal online procurement](https://www.gob.mx/cofepris/articulos/145739). Add these to the source lock before using them in learner copy.

## Brazil · Biolongeva · pt-BR

**Verified framework and time-sensitive update.** ANVISA's [product consultation](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/sistemas/consulta-a-registro) separates medicines and cosmetics. Its [2 July 2026 peptide notice](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/assuntos/noticias-anvisa/2026/checamos-peptideos-que-prometem-milagres-nao-estao-registrados-na-anvisa) states that products presented as GHK-Cu, BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295 and ipamorelin with health or aesthetic promises are not registered as medicines; BPC-157 and TB-500 are not authorized as supplements; injectable cosmetics do not exist. The notice describes individual prescription and a duly regularized compounding pharmacy as necessary conditions for compounding, not blanket permission for any substance. ANVISA's [cosmetic consultation](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/comunicacao/campanhas/estetica/consulta-de-cosmeticos) distinguishes products requiring registration from notified cosmetics. Its [September 2026 warning on home tirzepatide mixing](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br/assuntos/noticias-anvisa/manipulacao-caseira-de-tirzepatida-nao-e-segura) is relevant to the source's GLP-1 safety discussion.

## Indonesia · Parallaxnet Healthcare · id

**Verified framework.** BPOM's [Cek Produk portal](https://cekbpom.pom.go.id/) provides separate registered-product categories for medicines and cosmetics, and its [cosmetic search](https://cekbpom.pom.go.id/produk-kosmetika) allows checks by product, brand, composition and registration number. BPOM's [Cek KLIK guidance](https://www.pom.go.id/katabpom/cek-klik) asks users to check packaging, label, distribution authorization and expiry. The [BPOM legal repository](https://jdih.pom.go.id/) carries the current product-category and cosmetic-ingredient rules.

**Indonesia-specific status for m03, pending exact-product check.** [BPOM announced on 4 July 2026](https://www.pom.go.id/siaran-pers/bpom-dorong-akses-terapi-inovatif-untuk-penanganan-penyakit-metabolik-dengan-zat-aktif-tirzepatide) that tirzepatide had distribution authorization in February 2026 for type 2 diabetes and an added weight-management indication in July; it also says semaglutide and liraglutide medicines with similar indications have authorizations.
